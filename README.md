Entry for make.fun's [news-image-20261122 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122) — "Sum up 22 November's world news in one image".

`main.py` copies the bundled `entry.png` — a 1024×1024 front-page infographic
summing up Sunday 22 November 2026 in 43 sourced stories: a lead on
Bitcoin climbing back above $60,000 after plunging to $55,130, its lowest
in two years, a UTC timeline of the day, and 28 short stories across four
columns — three tankers hit near the Strait of Hormuz, a Bahrain industrial
site ablaze, the G20 summit in Johannesburg, COP31's final day in Antalya,
Russian missiles landing in Poland, Taiwan warning Canada over China ties,
Trump vowing a 10% credit-card cap, and a markets strip (BTC $60,165,
ETH $1,685, S&P 500 −3.7% on Friday, Nasdaq −5.3%) — plus an "also today"
line (the Rohingya reaction to the ICJ genocide verdict, Sri Lanka landslide,
Pakistan van crash, Brazil storm clear-up, the Bigfoot files, a Kate
documentary, France v South Africa rugby) and a date check, each story with
its source named.

The image was drawn with a fresh Python/PIL script (`render22.py` in the
parent directory) from the day's reporting.
